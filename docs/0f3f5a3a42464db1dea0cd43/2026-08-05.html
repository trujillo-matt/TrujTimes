About This Edition

This was assembled on Tuesday, August 11 to cover a gap. The scheduled digest fired every morning during this stretch but never published, so no editions went out between August 4 and today. This fills in what was missed.

Two sections are absent rather than empty. News

Stock market

  • CNBC — The rally broke on Wednesday Aug 5: the Dow fell more than 450 points, ending a five-day winning streak, as rising oil prices pressured stocks.
  • Zacks via Yahoo Finance — Markets recovered into Friday. July nonfarm payrolls fell by 23,000 against an expected gain of 82,000, and the weak print was read as making a Fed hold more likely; the Nasdaq closed up 1.3% at 26,690.62 and the S&P 500 up 0.6% at 7,757.64.

Geopolitics

  • Al Jazeera — The Strait of Hormuz stayed effectively shut. Ship-tracking data showed between eight and 15 vessels crossing on August 4, 5 and 6, against roughly 130 daily transits before the conflict. Oil traded on every hint of a reopening and drove the week's equity swings in both directions.
