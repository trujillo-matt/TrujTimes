News

Stock market

CNBC / Bloomberg — A sharp bond selloff dominated the week, with the 10-year Treasury yield pushing toward 4.75% and the 30-year climbing above 5.3%, its highest since 2007, on persistent inflation worries and elevated oil prices. The pressure pulled major indexes lower through midweek, leaving the S&P 500 down about 1.4% for the week even after stocks steadied Friday on a report that U.S. business activity grew at its fastest pace in more than four years. Tesla was the standout mover, on pace for its best week since May after The Information reported the company is preparing an August launch of its Cybercab robotaxi, a vehicle with no steering wheel; the stock gained roughly 5%. Gold extended its rebound from a second-quarter slump, rising nearly 5% on the week, while bitcoin climbed to around $77,000.

Geopolitics

Reuters / multiple outlets — Tensions with Iran escalated after a temporary ceasefire expired and President Trump ruled out extending it, telling reporters Iran is unlikely to accept the deal he wants and calling for its surrender; a senior Iranian official told Reuters that Tehran will shift to a "fully offensive" military posture with negotiations stalled. Trump separately threatened Oman over its role in reopening the Strait of Hormuz, even as reports suggested Iran and Oman are nearing an agreement on managing the strait. Israel struck the Abu al-Duhur military airbase in northwestern Syria with eight airstrikes early in the week, targeting the runway and storage facilities. Russia's embassy in London warned Britain of consequences after reports that British-made drones were used for the first time in long-range strikes on Russian territory. And in Africa, Zambian President Hakainde Hichilema was declared winner of a second term with 60% of the vote.

Technology

GeekWire / Tech Startups — Las Vegas opened its streets to thousands of robotaxis this week as regulators cleared the way for wider autonomous-vehicle operation, while chipmakers lined up tens of billions of dollars in fresh AI debt to fund buildouts. Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang met with the co-founder of Rebellions, a roughly $2.3 billion energy-efficient AI inference chip startup, to discuss a possible collaboration, investment, or acquisition. Apple is rumored to be developing camera-equipped AirPods aimed at giving its AI assistant the ability to see what the wearer sees. Chip startup Etched has reportedly surged to a $21 billion valuation, and robotaxi operator Pony.ai is expanding its overseas fleet plans. Amazon also said this week it plans to expand Prime Air drone delivery to nearly 500 U.S. cities and towns by the end of 2026, a roughly sixfold jump from its current 11-metro footprint.

AI

CNBC / AIToolsRecap — Anthropic reported second-quarter revenue of $10.9 billion, up 130%, and posted its first operating profit of $559 million — about two years ahead of its own internal schedule; the company is also rolling out machine-readable watermarks on AI-generated content to meet new European Union AI transparency rules taking effect this month. OpenAI's long-anticipated public S-1 filing is expected on SEC EDGAR any day, reportedly showing about $2 billion in monthly revenue against a projected $14 billion loss for 2026, with the company targeting a September stock market listing. OpenAI also cut pricing on its GPT 5.6 "Luna" model by roughly 80%, and ChatGPT reportedly crossed about one billion weekly active users in early August. Separately, Google granted Marvell a warrant for up to $12.2 billion of its stock as part of an expanded custom AI chip partnership that could bring Marvell roughly $120 billion in revenue through 2033. And Cloudflare launched Kitesurf, a lightweight browser runtime built for AI agents, alongside x402, a protocol letting agents pay for services autonomously, with more than 20 companies already participating.

Tech business, startups, and VC

Tech Startups / TechFundingNews — AI video platform Higgsfield raised a $400 million Series B at a $5.4 billion valuation, while critical-minerals company Mariana Minerals closed a $310 million Series B and semiconductor-and-AI-infrastructure startup Velaura AI raised $110 million in a Series A led by Seligman Ventures. Hypersonic weapons startup Castelion closed more than $1 billion in Series C funding at a reported $13 billion valuation, one of the week's largest rounds. Investors' message this week was unusually coherent: money is chasing companies that control hard-to-replicate infrastructure, data, manufacturing capacity, or regulated distribution, rather than another layer of AI software.

Golf equipment

Plugged In Golf — Callaway released its new Hi-Toe wedge line this week, offering five distinct grinds and two finishes across 50° to 60° lofts, forged from soft carbon steel with more aggressive saw-milled grooves for spin. Testers also highlighted the Incred Golf RFB SK1 putter for its forgiveness and ability to start putts on line, and the Parlinx P-1 Waterproof, a "super shoe" blending golf and running-shoe design. Pokémon investing

Pokémon Wizard / Cards N Packs — The tracked Pokémon TCG market, spanning 314 sets and more than 41,000 cards, rose 1.3% over the past week and 3.5% over the past month to a combined value of roughly $630,387. Barca Blaugranes — Barcelona has reportedly reached agreement with Lautaro Martínez on personal terms for a possible move to Camp Nou after the club's pursuit of Atlético Madrid's Julián Álvarez fell through, and is now preparing an initial offer of up to €100 million for Inter's captain. Minnesota Vikings

The Vikings opened their preseason with a 13-10 win over the New York Giants on August 15. ESPN / Daily Norseman — Carson Wentz's one-yard touchdown pass to Myles Price, a one-handed grab in the third quarter, proved the difference; Price finished with four catches for 51 yards. New starting quarterback Kyler Murray played just one series with the first-team offense, completing two of three passes for 10 yards, while Giants rookie Jaxson Dart threw a touchdown to Malachi Fields but was sacked twice, including once by Vikings safety Jay Ward. Odell Beckham Jr. made his return to the Giants organization to a big ovation, catching a 6-yard pass from Jameis Winston. The win came at a cost: All-Pro safety Jamal Adams is out for the season with a torn quad tendon, and third-round rookie tackle Caleb Tiernan is week-to-week with an ankle injury, though receiver Tai Felton has drawn praise for his camp performance. Minnesota hosts Baltimore today in preseason week two, with head coach Kevin O'Connell saying the first-team offense won't play again after joint practices with the Ravens.
