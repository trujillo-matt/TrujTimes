News

Stock market

The S&P 500 closed at a record 7,798.99 on Thursday, capping a week in which the index rose 3.6% and the Nasdaq gained 5.2% as chip stocks rebounded and cooling inflation data eased worries about another Federal Reserve rate hike. The Nasdaq Composite finished at 26,803.03, lifted by gains in Meta, Micron, and Netflix, while the Russell 2000 also notched a fresh record to close out the week. Reddit is set to join the S&P 500 before the opening bell on August 18. Separately, Anthropic investors are said to be targeting a valuation of at least $2 trillion, and by some estimates as high as $3 trillion, for an IPO as soon as October, resting on a previously unreported forecast of up to $200 billion in 2028 revenue. CNBC, Bloomberg, Reuters

Geopolitics

Middle East tensions stayed elevated this week as the US and Iran worked through Oman to resolve the standoff over the Strait of Hormuz, five months into the broader US-Israel-Iran war; a US naval blockade reimposed last month remains in place while talks continue. Houthi forces struck tankers in the Red Sea even as negotiators reported progress on route coordinates through Hormuz. Separately, Turkey, Pakistan, and Saudi Arabia signed a mutual defense pact, underscoring a broader realignment of Middle East security relationships. The US has also proposed transferring roughly $300 million in Turkish-held American weapons to Ukraine. Al Jazeera, CNN

Technology

Investment in AI's physical infrastructure kept accelerating this week, with billions flowing into Nvidia-backed data centers, next-generation networking, and custom chips, including a planned semiconductor factory from Tesla and SpaceX. AMD agreed to acquire Toronto-based AI chip startup Taalas, adding inference technology designed to cut the compute and memory overhead of running trained models as it looks for new ways to compete with Nvidia. Separately, Uber and Pony.ai are preparing to put more than 2,000 robotaxis on European roads. Tech Startups

AI

Anthropic is on track to report its first-ever quarterly operating profit, projecting $10.9 billion in Q2 revenue — more than double Q1 — and $559 million in operating income, as compute costs fell from 71 cents to 56 cents per revenue dollar. OpenAI is reportedly targeting an IPO as early as September, with a public S-1 filing expected within weeks. DARPA completed the first real-world flight of an F-16 fighter jet fully controlled by artificial intelligence. Google launched Gemini 3.7 Flash, pitched as its most capable workhorse model yet for coding and agent tasks, while Meta released an open-weight model called Glimmer that anyone can download and run locally. OpenAI paused internal development of its Astra model on August 7 after evaluations found it might be capable of autonomous zero-day exploit development. CNBC, Tech Startups

Golf equipment
