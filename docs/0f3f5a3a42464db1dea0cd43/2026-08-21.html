Newsletters

The Verge — Five new items: Genki launched the Manta on Kickstarter, a customizable wireless controller with a 2.79-inch touchscreen for adjusting profiles, stick tension, deadzones, and button mapping on the fly, alongside TMR joysticks, Hall-effect triggers, and up to 8,000Hz polling when wired. Google is rolling out a chat-based way to customize the Discover feed, letting users type or speak which topics they want more or less of and refine results through back-and-forth with an AI. Riot Games is ending active development on its fighting game 2XKO by the end of 2026 after failing to retain enough players, though servers stay online and purchases made through August 20 are being refunded. The Genesis GV90 NeoLun flagship EV SUV debuted with coach-style doors, a wood-veneer heated floor, a 23.6-inch pop-up center screen, a 123.5 kWh battery, and 657 horsepower, aiming for an early-2027 launch around $100,000. And Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg and his wife Priscilla Chan bought Strancally Castle, a roughly 16,000-square-foot Gothic-revival estate on 440 acres in Ireland's County Waterford, joining other tech billionaires who've bought large Irish estates in recent years.

Bless This Stuff — Five new items: German customization shop CROOKED unveiled its Scrambler 1200 Alps Edition, a bespoke adventure build on Triumph's Scrambler 1200 XE platform with a Dakar-style fairing and integrated lighting, weight trimmed by up to 20kg to roughly 210kg, and custom leather-and-Alcantara seating, with components also sold separately as a MotoKit. Pins & Aces showed off its modern-styled golf gear lineup — a rechargeable "LiquorStick," a premium bag, playful headcovers and ball markers, and gloves — aimed at players who want function without stuffy traditional looks. Adidas' Terrex Freehiker 3 Ultra GORE-TEX was highlighted as a hybrid hiking shoe splitting the difference between boot and trail runner, with a sock-like collar, cushioned midsole, a GORE-TEX membrane, and a Continental rubber outsole, priced at $220. The Kamaran is a catamaran-style inflatable kayak from an Argentine engineer that ditches the enclosed cockpit for two parallel PVC pontoons, weighs just 7kg, and packs down small enough for a car trunk or closet. And Logitech's RS H-Shifter is a 7-speed sim-racing shifter with a traditional H-pattern gate, a lockout for reverse and 7th gear, and contactless Hall-effect sensors, built to work across Logitech's PRO and RS50 wheel platforms.

Cool Material — One new piece, on a roughly 114-mile bike route billed as one of the best ways to see Washington state by rail trail; the article wasn't reachable to confirm further detail beyond the headline.

Uncrate — Five new items: a $15 million contemporary home in Pound Ridge, New York, designed around a glacial rock outcropping with glass, zinc-coated copper, and mirror-polished stainless steel, four bedrooms, a floating staircase, geothermal heating, and a 2,400-bottle wine cellar. Moncler and Clarks Originals teamed up on a GORE-TEX version of the classic 1968 Wallabee moccasin, swapping in a Vibram Trailgrip sole and waterproof suede lining while keeping the original silhouette, arriving in khaki or beige suede in early September. Koenigsegg's new CCGT1 is a road-legal tribute to its unraced 1960s CCGT race car, built on the CC850 chassis with a twin-turbo V8 making 1,280 hp on gasoline (1,600 hp on E85); all 70 units sold out within hours of its Monterey Car Week debut. A "Garb: Low Top" style guide built around Common Projects Achilles sneakers and a mechanical watch leaned on Levi's basics for an accessible classic-casual look. And Crown Royal is offering a 3-liter, 40%-ABV bottle of its Deluxe Canadian whisky as an oversized entertaining centerpiece, priced at $95 and shipping in the U.S. this November.
